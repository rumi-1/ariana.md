The **Nuristanis** live in the mountain valleys of Nuristan, in northeastern Afghanistan. Their languages form a separate branch of Indo-Iranian, neither fully Iranian nor Indo-Aryan, and highly diverse valley to valley.

The region was known before 1896 as Kafiristan ("land of unbelievers") for its pre-Islamic religion, distinct from the surrounding Muslim populations. It was forcibly converted to Islam under Amir Abdur Rahman Khan and renamed Nuristan, "land of light".
