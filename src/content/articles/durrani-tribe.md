The **Durrani** are a major [[Pashtuns|Pashtun]] tribal confederacy, based historically around [[Kandahar]] in southwestern Afghanistan. They were known as the Abdali until 1747, when [[Ahmad Shah Durrani]] took the title *Durr-i Durrān* ("pearl of pearls") on becoming ruler, and the confederacy was renamed after him.

This article is a stub.

## Rule

Durrani rulers, drawn mainly from the Popalzai and later Barakzai tribes, governed Afghanistan from 1747 until the monarchy's end in 1973, with brief interruptions.
