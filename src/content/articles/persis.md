**Persis** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It was the Achaemenid homeland, the province that gave its name to Persia, and the site of Persepolis and Pasargadae.

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to Fars, southwestern Iran.
