## Why the region is hard to study

Afghanistan lies at a crossroads between the Iranian plateau, Central Asia, and South Asia. Its mountains and its history of conquest and migration mean that many populations have mixed over long periods. Sampling is uneven: some valleys and communities have been studied in detail, while others have barely been sampled at all. Ongoing conflict has also made fieldwork difficult.

## What genetic data can show

Broad patterns of ancestry and population mixing can be estimated from modern and ancient DNA. These studies can sometimes indicate when groups diverged or mixed, and which regions contributed to their ancestry.

## What genetic data cannot show

Genetic data do not define a language, an ethnic group, or a nation. Languages can spread without large-scale population replacement, and ethnic identity is shaped by culture, history, and self-identification. Studies in this area have been used to support political claims about ancestry, and such claims go beyond what the data can support.
