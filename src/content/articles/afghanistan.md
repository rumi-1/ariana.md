**Afghanistan** is a landlocked country in Central and South Asia. Its neighbours are Iran to the west, Pakistan to the south and east, China to the far northeast, and Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, and Turkmenistan to the north.

This stub records only the basics. The country's history, languages, and peoples are covered in their own articles.

## Geography

Most of the country is mountainous. The [[Hindu Kush]] runs across the centre and northeast, and the land falls toward the deserts of the southwest. Its rivers, including the Amu Darya on the northern border, feed the irrigated lands on which most people depend.
