**Ferdowsi** was a poet born near the town of Tus in [[Khorasan]]. He is best known as the author of the *Shahnameh* ("Book of Kings"), a long verse epic that retells the history of Iran from mythical times to the Arab conquest.

## Life

Dates for Ferdowsi are approximate. Most sources place his birth around 940 and his death around 1020. He worked under the patronage of local rulers, and the traditional accounts of his later years, including the story of his reward from the Ghaznavid court, are uncertain.

## The Shahnameh

The *Shahnameh* contains roughly 50,000 couplets, and Ferdowsi says he completed it around 1010. It draws on older prose chronicles and oral material, and it shaped the Persian literary language for centuries afterward.

## Legacy

The poem is central to Persian identity and is read across [[Afghanistan]], Iran, Tajikistan, and beyond. Its language, which avoids many Arabic loanwords, is an important source for the development of [[Dari]] and modern Persian.
