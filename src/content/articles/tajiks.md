The **Tajiks** are a Persian-speaking people. In Afghanistan they are concentrated in the northeast, including the Panjshir valley and Badakhshan, and in the western city of [[Herat]]; across the border, Tajiks form the majority population of Tajikistan and a large minority in Uzbekistan.

Dari, the Afghan variety of Persian, is their native language, and much of the classical Persian literary tradition is claimed as a shared heritage.
