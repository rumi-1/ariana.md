**Hotak** is a tribe of the [[Ghilzai]] Pashtun confederacy. Under Mirwais Hotak, it led a 1709 uprising against Safavid rule at [[Kandahar]], and his son Mahmud went on to conquer Isfahan and briefly rule much of Iran, before the dynasty collapsed and the [[Durrani]] rose in its place.

This article is a stub. Mirwais Hotak does not yet have his own article.
