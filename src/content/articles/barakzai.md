**Barakzai** is a tribe of the [[Durrani]] Pashtun confederacy. Its Mohammadzai branch succeeded the Popalzai as Afghanistan's ruling line in the 19th century, beginning with Dost Mohammad Khan, and held the throne until the monarchy ended in 1973.
