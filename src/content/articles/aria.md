**Aria** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It covered the valley of the Hari Rud around Artacoana and the city later refounded as Alexandria Ariana — [[Herat]].

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to Herat, western Afghanistan.
