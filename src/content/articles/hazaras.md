The **Hazaras** are a people of central Afghanistan, concentrated in the mountainous region known as Hazarajat, around [[Bamiyan]]. They speak Hazaragi, a dialect of Dari, and are predominantly Shia Muslim in a country that is majority Sunni, which has shaped their modern political and social history.

Claims about Hazara origins, including popular claims of direct Mongol descent, are debated; see [[Genetic history of Afghanistan]] for what the evidence can and cannot establish.
