**Arachosia** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It ran from the Helmand river to the Indus, around Alexandria Arachosia — [[Kandahar]].

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to southern Afghanistan and the Pashtun belt of Pakistan.
