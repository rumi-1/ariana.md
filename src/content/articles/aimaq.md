The **Aimaq** (from a Mongolian-Turkic word for "tribe") are a cluster of historically semi-nomadic, Persian-speaking tribes of west-central Afghanistan, traditionally grouped as the Chahar ("Four") Aimaq. Their origins are mixed, combining Iranian, Turkic, and Mongol elements, and their exact tribal composition is described differently by different sources.
