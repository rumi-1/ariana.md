The **Ghilzai** are one of the largest [[Pashtuns|Pashtun]] tribal confederacies, concentrated in eastern and southeastern Afghanistan around [[Ghazni]] and further south. They have historically rivalled the [[Durrani]] for power.

This article is a stub.

## History

A Ghilzai dynasty, the Hotak, briefly ruled from Kandahar and then from Isfahan after overthrowing the Safavid Empire in 1722, before the Durrani rose to dominance in 1747.
