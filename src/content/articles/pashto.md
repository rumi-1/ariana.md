**Pashto** is an Eastern Iranian language. It is the main language of the [[Pashtuns]] and one of the two official languages of [[Afghanistan]], alongside [[Dari]]. It is also widely spoken in northwestern Pakistan.

## Classification

Pashto belongs to the Eastern branch of the Iranian languages, which also includes Ossetic, Pamir languages, and Yaghnobi. It is therefore more distantly related to Persian than the Western Iranian languages are to each other, though all of them share a common ancestry.

## Script

Pashto is written with an adapted form of the Arabic script, with additional letters for sounds that Arabic does not have.

## Dialects

Pashto has several main dialect groups. The most important distinction is between the southern and northern varieties, which differ in pronunciation and some vocabulary. The exact classification is debated among linguists.
