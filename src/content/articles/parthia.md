**Parthia** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It later gave its name to the Parthian Empire: the Arsacid dynasty that ruled Iran from around 247 BCE to 224 CE traced its origins here.

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to Khorasan, northeastern Iran.
