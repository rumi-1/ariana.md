**Hyrcania** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It covered the fertile southeastern shore of the Caspian Sea; the name comes from Old Persian *Varkāna*, "land of wolves".

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to Golestan and Mazandaran, Iran.
