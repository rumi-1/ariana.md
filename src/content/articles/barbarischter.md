This article is a joke. It is kept here as a running gag, not as history; nothing below should be cited anywhere else.

**Barbarischter** is a figure of uncertain date, uncertain province, and uncertain species, claimed by family tradition to be of Kalakani stock — a line traced, so the story goes, to the village of Kalakan north of Kabul. No document of any period mentions him by name, which his descendants take as proof of how thoroughly he avoided paperwork.

## Reputation

Accounts agree on little except his ferocity of mind. He is said to have out-argued a sitting vizier on three separate occasions before the vizier realised an argument was taking place, and to have once held a position in debate for eleven hours on a point nobody present could later reconstruct. His beard, in every description, is given its own clause.

## Appearance

The sole surviving portrait, reproduced above, is disputed on every count: the sitting, the artist, and the decade. Some family members hold that it shows him at the height of his powers; others maintain it shows someone else's dog.
