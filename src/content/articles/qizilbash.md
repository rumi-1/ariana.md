The **Qizilbash** trace their presence in Afghanistan to Safavid and, later, Nader Shah-era Turkic soldiers and administrators, many of whom settled in Kabul, Herat, and Kandahar and became part of the ruling and clerical administration under the Durrani state. Over time the community largely shifted to speaking Dari and has become harder to distinguish statistically from the wider urban population.
