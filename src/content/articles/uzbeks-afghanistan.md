The **Uzbeks** of Afghanistan live mainly in the northern provinces bordering Uzbekistan, including around [[Balkh]] and [[Mazar-i-Sharif]]. They speak Uzbek, a Turkic language, and are traditionally associated with farming, trade, and horsemanship.
