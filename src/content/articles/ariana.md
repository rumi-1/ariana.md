**Ariana** is a classical name for the eastern part of the Iranian plateau. It covers roughly the land of present-day [[Afghanistan]], with its edges extending into neighbouring areas. The name is used by Greek and Roman writers, and it has older roots in the traditions of the Iranian peoples.

## Name and origin

The word goes back to the Old Iranian term *Aryana*, which is related to the self-designation *Arya* of Iranian-speaking peoples. In the Zoroastrian [[Avesta]], the original homeland is called *Airyana Vaējah*, usually rendered "the seed of the Aryans".

Greek writers adopted the name as *Ariana*. In their usage it referred to a region east of [[Persia]] and north of the Indian frontier, and not to a single state.

## Geography

The region is dominated by the [[Hindu Kush]] mountains and the rivers that flow from them into the basins of the north and the south. The climate is arid to semi-arid, and settlement has always clustered along rivers and oases.

## Later use

After the Greek period, the land was known under other names as successive empires and dynasties controlled it. The older term survived in learned writing and in the names of places and institutions. Today, the name is used mainly in scholarship and by people who want a name for the region that is not tied to a single modern state.
