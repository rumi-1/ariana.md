**Drangiana** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It covered the Helmand river delta and the Hamun lakes; the name survives in Sistan, itself a contraction of "Sakastan", after later Saka settlement.

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to Sistan, in Iran and Afghanistan.
