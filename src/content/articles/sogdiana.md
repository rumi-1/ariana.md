**Sogdiana** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It lay beyond the Oxus, centred on Maracanda (Samarkand) and the merchant cities of the Sogdians, whose trade network later anchored much of the Silk Road.

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to Uzbekistan and Tajikistan.
