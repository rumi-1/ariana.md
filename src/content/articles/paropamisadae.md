**Paropamisadae** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It covered the Hindu Kush and the Kabul valley; Alexandria in the Caucasus, one of the many cities Alexander founded, stood near what is now [[Begram]].

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to Kabul, Bamiyan, and the Hindu Kush.
