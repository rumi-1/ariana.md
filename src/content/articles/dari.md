**Dari** is the Afghan variety of [[Persian]]. Its name is traditionally derived from the word for "court", a reference to the Persian-speaking courts of the region. It is one of the two official languages of [[Afghanistan]], alongside [[Pashto]].

## Relation to Persian

Dari is mutually intelligible with the Persian of Iran, and with Tajik in Central Asia. Differences are mostly in pronunciation, vocabulary, and some grammar. Whether these are dialects of one language or separate languages depends on the criteria used, and the question is often decided for political reasons.

## Literature

Much of the classic Persian literature of the region was written in areas where Dari is spoken today. The poet [[Ferdowsi]] wrote in the Khorasan tradition, and the cities of [[Khorasan]] were centres of Persian learning.

## Official status

Dari became an official language of Afghanistan with the constitution adopted in 2004, alongside Pashto. The two languages are used in government, education, and the media.
