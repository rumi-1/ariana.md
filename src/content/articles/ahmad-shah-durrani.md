**Ahmad Shah Durrani** was a [[Pashtuns|Pashtun]] military leader and the founder of the Durrani Empire. He was chosen as ruler at Kandahar in 1747, and his state is generally regarded as the beginning of the modern [[Afghanistan]].

## Rise to power

Ahmad Shah came from the Abdali tribe, which was later renamed Durrani. He served under the Persian ruler Nader Shah, and after Nader Shah's death in 1747 he was chosen by a council of Pashtun leaders. He took the title *Durr-i Durrān* ("Pearl of the Pearls"), from which the name Durrani comes.

## The empire

Over his reign Ahmad Shah extended his rule across [[Khorasan]], much of present-day Afghanistan, parts of Iran, and the Punjab and Kashmir regions of the Indian subcontinent. The empire was held together by alliances and tribute, and it did not become a fully centralised state.

## Legacy

Ahmad Shah is honoured in Afghanistan as the father of the nation. The dynasty he founded ruled into the early 19th century, and the name Durrani continues to be used for the Pashtun confederation that produced him.
