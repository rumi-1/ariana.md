**Margiana** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It was the oasis of the Murghab river delta around the city of Merv, known to the Greeks as Antiochia Margiana.

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to Mary (Merv), Turkmenistan.
