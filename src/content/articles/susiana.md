**Susiana** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It was the lowland province of Elam around Susa, one of the Achaemenid capitals and the administrative centre for much of the empire's correspondence.

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to Khuzestan, southwestern Iran.
