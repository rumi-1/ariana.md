**Gedrosia** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It covered the arid Makran coast, crossed at great cost by Alexander's army in 325 BCE on the march back from India.

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to the Makran coast and Balochistan, in Iran and Pakistan.
