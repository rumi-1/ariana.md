**Gandhara** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It lay east of the Khyber Pass. Its language, Gāndhārī, was written in the Kharoṣṭhī script; Dardic languages survive today in the surrounding valleys.

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to the Peshawar valley, Swat, and Taxila in northwestern Pakistan, and eastern Afghanistan.
