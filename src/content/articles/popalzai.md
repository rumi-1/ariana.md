**Popalzai** is a tribe of the [[Durrani]] Pashtun confederacy. [[Ahmad Shah Durrani]], who founded the Durrani Empire at [[Kandahar]] in 1747, belonged to this tribe, and Popalzai leaders held a central place in Durrani politics for generations afterward.
