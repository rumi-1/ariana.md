The **Hindu Kush** is a mountain range that runs roughly east to west through the centre and northeast of [[Afghanistan]], and continues into northern Pakistan. Its highest peaks rise above 7,000 metres.

## Significance

For most of history the range has been a barrier and a route at the same time. Its passes linked the northern plains of Central Asia with the valleys of the south, and the mountains shaped settlement, language, and political boundaries.
