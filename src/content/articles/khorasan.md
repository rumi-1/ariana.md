**Khorasan** is a historical region on the eastern edge of the Iranian plateau. The name comes from Middle Persian *khvarāsān*, usually glossed as "land of the rising sun", or "the east". Its meaning shifted over time, and so did its boundaries.

## Extent

At its greatest extent in the medieval period, Khorasan covered much of northeastern Iran, most of present-day [[Afghanistan]], and parts of Central Asia. Its modern territory is divided among several countries. In Iran, it is still the name of several provinces, and the city of Mashhad is the capital of Razavi Khorasan.

## Major cities

Historically important cities include [[Balkh]] in the north of present-day Afghanistan, [[Herat]] in the west, Nishapur, and Merv. Many of them were centres of learning, trade, and religious life along the routes that linked China, India, and the Mediterranean.

## Culture

Khorasan is closely tied to Persian literature. The poet [[Ferdowsi]] was born near Tus in the region. Its cities also produced scholars in philosophy, medicine, and science.
