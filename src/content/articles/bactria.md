**Bactria** was one of the satrapies (provinces) of the Achaemenid Persian Empire in its eastern reaches. It lay between the Hindu Kush and the Amu Darya (Oxus), with its capital at Bactra, the city known today as [[Balkh]].

This article is a stub. Satrapy boundaries were never fixed lines on the ground, and they shifted across the Achaemenid, Hellenistic, and later periods; what is given here is approximate, after the classical geographers.

## Today

Its territory corresponds roughly to northern Afghanistan, southern Uzbekistan and Tajikistan.
